# SUBMISSION STORY — PROJECT MORRO: THE MAYBE-TO-MAGIC EXPERIENCE
## HAX Baby Hackathon — Track 2: Your Own Baby (Original Character)

### 1. The Story Behind the Build
Every creator knows the quiet weight of "Maybe tomorrow." When we postpone the things we care about, that hesitation does not simply vanish — it lingers in the air like a cloudy sigh. We created **Morro, the Little Star Prince**, as a joyful, kind-hearted companion who never shames you for putting things off. Instead, Morro is a rare magical SHiko born from the stars who catches those drifting "Maybe" particles, gently breaks them down into one tiny, lovable step, and rewards your effort with a glowing star for your personal Done Constellation.

Our proudest achievement is the **Maybe-to-Magic Ritual** — a fully interactive, end-to-end journey rather than a decorative mockup. Visitors don't just read about Morro's mission; they type in what they've been putting off, watch the dark Maybe cloud fly into Morro's pouch with cheerful audio chimes, receive an achievable micro-step, start a genuine drift-free Star Sprint timer, and earn an immutable star in their celestial chart.

---

### 2. Official 5-Point Character Sheet (Track 2 Verification)
1. **Name**: Morro (The Little Star Prince)
2. **What They Are**: A rare magical creature called a **SHiko**, born from converged starlight. Morro has fluffy white-and-blue fur, sparkling sapphire eyes, fox-like ears, dragon horns tipped in gold, and a magical fluffy tail. He is accompanied by **Sparky**, his cheerful blue spirit friend. Morro's personality is playful, curious, kind, cheerful, and adventurous.
3. **What They Do**: Morro catches the negative energy released from postponed tasks ("Maybes") and transmutes it into positive, guilt-free reminders and bite-sized actions that anyone can start right now.
4. **Their Story**: As centuries of postponed dreams accumulated in the cosmos, they swirled into a starlit storm. Rather than bringing darkness, the converged energy sparkled into a radiant star that gave birth to Morro. Now, Morro and Sparky travel between desks and workshops everywhere, turning hesitation into starlight.
5. **Tagline**: *"Make the Work, Done Today"* (Supporting slogan: *"Turn Maybe Into Done"*).

---

### 3. The Three Flagship Portfolio Projects
1. **The Maybe Catcher**: A magical transmuter instrument that captures postponement signals and breaks down intimidating tasks into comfortable micro-actions.
2. **The Star Sprint Engine**: A precision focus timer (5 min, 15 min, 25 min) built on drift-free browser time calculation with gentle ambient audio pulses and celebratory completion fireworks.
3. **The Done Constellation**: An interactive celestial observatory mapping every completed session and task into connected starry sky nodes with local persistence.

---

### 4. Interactive Immersion & Hidden Easter Egg
- **Immersion**: Morro's eyes and head smoothly track the user's cursor across the screen via mathematical coordinate interpolation, accompanied by a celestial Day/Night mode toggle that shifts the palette from warm starlight cream to deep cosmic midnight.
- **Hidden Easter Egg**: Tapping Sparky the Spirit Companion 3 times in quick succession (or finding the hidden golden star in the constellation map) unlocks **Morro's Secret Celestial Tea Party & Starlight Blessing**, revealing exclusive lore, an ambient music-box lullaby, and a special badge!
